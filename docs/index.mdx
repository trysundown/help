# Sundown licence help: activating, reinstalling and moving between builds

Sundown is a Mac menu bar app made by Jack Richards that warms the display from 6500 K down to 500 K in 100 K steps, dims it, and follows a schedule you set. This article covers the licence side: how a key gets into the app, what happens when you reinstall, and which build your key works with. The activation and recovery details below describe the main Sundown build up to 1.5.3, and the section on builds also covers Sundown Classic 1.6.0.

## Activating your key

The licence window opens on the same sunset photo as the welcome screen. Enter your key and choose Activate, and the button fills up while Sundown checks it. Version 1.2.1 added two shortcuts. If you copy your key before opening Sundown, the activation field fills itself in. And the Open in Sundown link on the website activates the licence directly, so there is nothing to paste at all.

## If the check fails

If Sundown cannot reach the licence lookup for a moment, that no longer counts as a rejection. That fix arrived in 1.5.2, so on an older version a brief network problem could look like a bad key. Since 1.5.1, licence checks have also been gentler and faster, and the release notes are explicit that no one who has paid is locked out.

## Reinstalling or resetting your Mac

Since 1.4.2, reinstalling keeps you licensed automatically. In 1.4.3 the same recovery was extended to complimentary and VIP licences, which now get their key back after a reinstall or a Keychain reset like everyone else.

## Which build your key works with

The main Sundown build needs macOS 14 or later. On macOS 12 Monterey or 13 Ventura you use Sundown Classic 1.6.0, and your existing licence works there too, with the same account and the same key. Both builds are universal, so they run on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Copying and pasting into the key field

If paste did nothing in the key field on an early version, that was fixed in 1.4.3, where Command C, Command V, Command X and Command A started working everywhere in the app, the licence field included.

Both builds are on [Sundown](https://trysundown.com). If you are still comparing apps before you buy, [Sundown vs f.lux on Mac](https://trysundown.com/vs/flux) sets the two side by side, and the [Blue Light Calculator](https://trysundown.com/tools/blue-light-calculator) is a free estimate of the blue light from your screen after sunset, built from your device, your screen hours after sunset, any filter you use and your bedtime.
